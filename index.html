🫀 The Atlas of Human Feeling

A Complete Guide to Every Emotion and Everything About Them
by Walusimbi Leon (SGSS)

About

The Atlas of Human Feeling is a comprehensive exploration of the full spectrum of human emotion — from the primary six (joy, sadness, anger, fear, surprise, disgust) to the complex social and moral emotions (love, jealousy, guilt, shame, pride, empathy, nostalgia, awe, boredom, hope, despair, and many others).

For each emotion: what it feels like from the inside, what triggers it, what it does to the body and brain, and what science and philosophy reveal about its nature. Plus chapters on neuroscience, emotional intelligence, emotional contagion, culture, mental illness, and the art of feeling well.
